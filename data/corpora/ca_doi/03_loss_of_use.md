# California Coverage D minimum: 24 months following declared disasters

Cal. Ins. Code §2051.5 establishes that Loss-of-Use (Coverage D, Additional Living Expense and fair rental value) coverage must run for at least 24 months following a state-declared catastrophe disaster, and at least 12 months for non-catastrophe losses. This is a statutory floor; carriers may write longer terms but not shorter.

**Why 24 months matters in CA.** Major wildfire events (Camp Fire 2018, Tubbs 2017, Maui 2023, Park Fire 2024) routinely involve rebuild timelines of 18-30 months even with insurance fully in place - permitting, code-upgraded materials, contractor backlogs, and lot-line / setback retesting all extend the timeline. A 12-month Cov D limit leaves the insured paying for temporary housing out of pocket from month 13 onward; the 24-month statutory minimum substantially mitigates that gap.

**ALE eligibility.** Coverage D pays the necessary increase in living expenses while the dwelling is uninhabitable due to a covered loss. "Necessary" is key - it pays the gap above the insured's normal cost of living, not the full temporary-housing rent. Receipts and documentation are required.

**Fair rental value.** When the home is rented to a tenant, Cov D pays the lost rental income (less expenses that would not continue) for the same 24/12-month period.

**Stacking with FAIR Plan + DIC.** FAIR Plan does not include Coverage D; the DIC wrap supplies it. The Coverage Agent must verify the DIC's Cov D term is at least 24 months for CA-declared-disaster perils.

**Common follow-up question patterns.**
- "How long does ALE last after a wildfire?" -> 24 months minimum per §2051.5; longer if the policy specifies.
- "Will my ALE pay for the same square footage I had?" -> Generally no - "comparable" not "equivalent" is the standard. Document everything.
- "What if rebuild takes longer than 24 months?" -> Insureds may request extensions; carriers commonly grant 6-12 month extensions for catastrophe events with documented contractor backlogs. The DIC carrier and the admitted Cov D writer must align if separate.
