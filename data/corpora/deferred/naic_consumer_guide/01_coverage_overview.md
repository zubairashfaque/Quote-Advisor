# Standard homeowners coverages (HO-3 multi-peril form)

A typical HO-3 personal lines homeowners policy carries six discrete coverage parts (Coverage A through F) plus optional endorsements. Each is a separate insuring agreement with its own limit, deductible, and exclusions.

**Coverage A - Dwelling.** Insures the residence structure, attached structures (e.g., attached garage), and built-in fixtures. Settlement is on a replacement-cost basis when the limit is at least 80% of replacement cost; otherwise actual-cash-value with depreciation. Lender-backed mortgages typically require Coverage A to equal at least the lesser of replacement cost value (RCV) or unpaid principal balance (UPB).

**Coverage B - Other Structures.** Detached structures - sheds, fences, detached garages, storage barns. Default limit is 10% of Coverage A; can be scheduled higher for properties with significant outbuildings.

**Coverage C - Personal Property (Contents).** Furniture, electronics, appliances, clothing, and most other movable belongings. Default limit is 50-70% of Coverage A. Subject to special limits on cash, jewelry, firearms, business property; scheduled personal property endorsements raise individual-item limits.

**Coverage D - Loss of Use (Additional Living Expense).** Reimburses additional living expense and fair rental value when the dwelling is uninhabitable due to a covered loss. Default limit is 20-30% of Coverage A. California requires a 24-month minimum following declared disasters per Cal. Ins. Code §2051.5.

**Coverage E - Personal Liability.** Defends and indemnifies the insured against bodily injury or property damage claims arising from premises liability or personal acts (excluding business and auto). Standard limits are $100K, $300K, $500K. Pool, dog, trampoline, and other "attractive nuisance" exposures often increase recommended limits.

**Coverage F - Medical Payments to Others.** No-fault medical payments coverage for guests, regardless of negligence. Default $1K-$5K per person.

Endorsements modify or extend these baseline coverages. Common ones: water backup of sewers and drains, scheduled personal property (jewelry, fine art), ordinance-or-law (increased cost from building-code compliance), service line, identity theft, and equipment breakdown.
