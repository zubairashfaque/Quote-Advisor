# Defensible Space Zone 0 - the Ember-Resistant Zone (0-5 feet)

California Public Resources Code §4291 mandates 100 feet of defensible space around every habitable structure in a State Responsibility Area (SRA) wildfire zone. Effective January 1, 2024, AB 3074 (2020) added a new innermost zone - "Zone 0", the Ember-Resistant Zone - covering 0 to 5 feet from the structure.

**Zone 0 - the most stringent.** The 5-ft band immediately surrounding the home must be free of ALL combustible materials. The intent is that wind-borne embers landing within 5 feet of the structure should find nothing to ignite.

**Specifically prohibited in Zone 0.**

- **Wood mulch, bark mulch, pine straw.** Replace with rock, gravel, decomposed granite, or other non-combustible groundcover.
- **Combustible fences (wood) attaching directly to the home.** A 5-ft non-combustible break (metal gate, masonry section) must separate any wood fence from the structure.
- **Vegetation - shrubs, vines, woody groundcovers, trees.** Limited to widely-spaced ornamental plants in pots; no continuous fuel beds.
- **Stored combustibles - firewood, lumber, pallets, propane tanks.** Must be relocated outside Zone 0.
- **Outdoor furniture cushions and umbrellas with synthetic fabrics.** Removed during fire weather or replaced with metal/wicker / fire-rated alternatives.
- **Vegetation overhanging from outside Zone 0** (e.g., an oak branch reaching across into the 5-ft band) - must be limbed back.

**Permitted in Zone 0.**

- Hardscape (concrete, stone, brick, decomposed granite paths).
- Non-combustible fencing (steel, wrought iron, masonry).
- Potted plants on non-combustible bases (avoid clustering).
- Inorganic mulch (rock, gravel) up to 4 inches deep.

**Insurance interaction.** Insurers writing CA homeowners under the Sustainable Insurance Strategy 2024 framework typically require Zone 0 compliance documentation as a precondition for any wildfire-mitigation premium discount. Some carriers offer 5-15% premium reductions for documented full Zone 0 + Zone 1 compliance with ongoing inspection.

**Verification.** CAL FIRE and most local fire authorities will conduct a defensible space inspection on request; the inspection report is the evidence carriers ask for. Inspections are typically free; reports remain valid 12-24 months.

**Common follow-up question.** "I removed my wood mulch and put gravel in the 5-ft band - does that lower my premium?" The Follow-up agent should retrieve this chunk, confirm the alignment with Zone 0 requirements, and surface the carrier-discount pathway (request a defensible space inspection certificate, submit to the carrier, request rate review at next renewal).
