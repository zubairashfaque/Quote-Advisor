# Non-structural flood mitigation - low-cost, high-leverage steps

Not every flood-mitigation conversation should jump to elevation. FEMA P-312 chapter 7 catalogues non-structural retrofits that materially reduce flood-claim severity at a fraction of the cost of structural changes. The Follow-up agent should reach for these first when the customer's question is framed economically.

**Top non-structural retrofits, by ROI.**

1. **Elevate utilities and appliances.** HVAC, water heater, electrical panel, washer/dryer above BFE. Cost: $2,500-$10,000. Premium impact: 10-15% NFIP reduction. Claim impact: 60-80% reduction in losses to elevated equipment.

2. **Install flood vents in enclosed crawlspaces.** Required by NFIP for AE-zone enclosures: 1 sqin of net free vent area per sqft of enclosed space, vents within 1 ft of grade on at least two walls. Cost: $50-$150 per vent. Without vents, NFIP rates the enclosure as a basement.

3. **Backwater valves on sewer laterals.** Prevents sewer surcharge from flooding the basement during heavy rain. Cost: $300-$700. Not strictly a flood-zone issue but reduces sewer-backup losses; pairs with the water-backup endorsement.

4. **Sump pump with battery backup.** Continuous removal of seepage and minor flood water. Cost: $1,200-$2,500 installed. Most homeowner policies require the home have one before water-damage coverage is enforced.

5. **Anchor the fuel tank.** Above-ground oil tanks float and rupture in flood; anchored tanks survive. Cost: $300-$1,000. Substantial environmental claim avoidance.

6. **Seal walls and floors against minor seepage.** Hydraulic cement, polyurethane crack injection. Cost: $500-$3,000. Modest impact on major flood losses; meaningful on chronic minor seepage.

7. **Move valuables to upper floors / install raised shelving in basement.** Zero structural cost; meaningful contents-claim severity reduction.

**Stacking with insurance.** Non-structural retrofits don't typically reset the NFIP rate class on their own (other than equipment elevation), but they materially reduce claim frequency and severity. Over a 10-year window, the cumulative deductible savings often exceed the retrofit cost.

**The conversation flow.** Customer asks "what can I do about my flood premium?" -> Follow-up agent suggests the elevation + Elevation Certificate path (P-312-ELEV-01) for structural change AND the equipment-elevation / flood-vent path (this chunk) for the cheaper, faster wins.
