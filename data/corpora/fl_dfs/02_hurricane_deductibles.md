# Florida hurricane deductibles - the four statutorily-mandated options

Personal residential homeowners policies in Florida must offer the insured a choice among four hurricane deductibles, codified at Fla. Stat. §627.701. The Coverage Agent must surface all four at quote time; the insured's selection is captured on the policy declarations page.

**The four options.**

1. **$500 flat dollar.** Available only when the dwelling limit is $250,000 or less. Carries the highest premium factor (~1.40× of the 5% factor) because it is a small absolute deductible.
2. **2% of Coverage A.** Mid-low deductible; premium factor ~1.20×.
3. **5% of Coverage A.** The default Florida hurricane deductible; premium factor 1.00× (the rating reference).
4. **10% of Coverage A.** Highest deductible; premium factor ~0.85×.

**What "hurricane" means under §627.4025.** A hurricane deductible applies only to losses caused by a NAMED TROPICAL CYCLONE that has been classified as a hurricane by the National Hurricane Center, and only during the period beginning 12 hours before landfall in Florida and ending 72 hours after the storm is downgraded below tropical-storm status. Outside this window, only the all-other-perils deductible applies.

**Per-occurrence vs. annual.** Florida hurricane deductibles are applied per-CALENDAR-YEAR, not per-storm. After the first hurricane loss in a year, subsequent named-storm losses use the (lower) all-other-perils deductible. This is one of the strongest consumer protections in the §627.701 framework.

**Mortgage interaction.** Lenders often require the all-other-perils deductible be no higher than $1,000 - $2,500 to satisfy mortgage-payable provisions. The hurricane deductible is treated separately and lenders generally accept up to 5% of Coverage A; some accept 10% with documented escrow.

**Sinkhole and CGCC are separate.** Catastrophic Ground Cover Collapse (CGCC) is mandatory under §627.706 and uses the all-other-perils deductible. Optional Sinkhole Loss coverage (also §627.706) typically uses a separate 10% sinkhole deductible.

**The Counterfactual agent's "raise my deductible" path.** When the customer asks "what if I raised the deductible to 5K?", the Counterfactual agent applies the appropriate FL hurricane-deductible premium factor and re-runs the Pricing chain on the forked state. It must also surface the eligibility constraint: $500 flat is only available when dwelling <= $250K.
